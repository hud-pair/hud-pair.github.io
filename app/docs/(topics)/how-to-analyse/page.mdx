## What Explorer does

Explorer ranks every property in a portfolio by retrofit priority. It's not a black box: every
recommendation ships with a **confidence score** and a feature importance breakdown, so an asset
manager can see *why* a property was ranked the way it was, not just the ranking itself.

<Callout tone="warning">
  Explorer prioritises, it doesn't decide. Treat its output as a starting point for a survey, not
  a substitute for one.
</Callout>

## Reading a recommendation

For the pilot portfolio, **classification, hot water, secondary heating, and wall type** are
consistently the strongest predictors. A worked example — 39 Newsome Road, Huddersfield — is
covered in the [pilot portfolio case study](/case-studies/pilot-portfolio-epc-uplift):

- Current rating: band D (62)
- Potential rating: band B (86)
- Estimated CO2 saving: 6.20 to 2.10 tonnes a year

### Confidence scores

Every recommendation returns a confidence score alongside the ranking. A low score usually means
the property's data is incomplete (missing wall type or heating fields) rather than that the
recommendation itself is weak — worth checking the underlying EPC data in
[How to use](/docs/how-to-use) before acting on it.
